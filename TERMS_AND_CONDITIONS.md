## 2. Description of Services

KiddoLog provides a centralized platform for schools, teachers, and parents to manage and share information about children, including:

- **Attendance Management:** Student and staff check-in/check-out tracking, including QR code scanning and health screening.
- **Activity Logging:** Recording daily activities such as meals, naps, learning sessions, medication administration, and more.
- **Notices and Announcements:** Sharing school-wide or class-specific announcements, alerts, and events.
- **School and Class Management:** Managing school details, classrooms, staff, and student enrollments.
- **Staff Directory:** Viewing and managing school staff information.
- **Invitations:** Inviting parents, teachers, and administrators to join the platform.
